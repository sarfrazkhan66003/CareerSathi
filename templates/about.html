ABOUT CAREERSATHI

Real guidance. Better decisions.

Guiding Futures, Building Careers — a student-first career guidance initiative for India.

FOUNDER & VISION

Mohammad Sarfraz Khan
Founder & Developer, CareerSathi

CareerSathi is the startup concept and business plan developed by Mohammad Sarfraz Khan. Its goal is to help learners, especially students in Classes 8–12, choose educational and career pathways with more clarity, authentic mentorship and practical exposure.

Our focus is transparency: explaining programmes, sharing community experiences with moderation, and making educational guidance easier to access beyond major metropolitan cities.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

From a student's first question to a confident next step.

Career assessments, college comparisons, school-level awareness, mentorship enquiries, self-study roadmaps and practical career resources.

For mentors & professionals

Apply for a profile; visibility requires team verification. Guidance should be accurate and never promise admission or jobs.
