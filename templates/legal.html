Information collected

We collect account details (name, email, user role and optional profile data), booking requests, course registrations, reviews, enquiries and a pseudonymous browser identifier for aggregate visit analytics. We use it to deliver CareerSathi services and administer the platform.

Account controls

Accounts may be disabled by administrators for safety and moderation. Contact the service operator to request access, correction or deletion. A documented process for handling requests must be established before launch.

Children's privacy

CareerSathi serves school-age users. Before opening registration to minors, implement age verification, appropriate parent/guardian consent, purpose-limited processing and all locally applicable data protection safeguards. Do not deploy publicly for children until these controls are completed.

Third parties and retention

Hosting and operational providers may process information. Final vendor disclosures, retention periods, contact address and legal basis require confirmation before launch.
